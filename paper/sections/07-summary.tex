\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:summary}
% ==============================================================================

Fast CASPaxos is a small extension to CASPaxos.
The piggybacked prepare is reinterpreted as an atomic promise for the next ballot, which may be either proposer-owned or shared.
That is enough to add Fast Paxos-style leaderless fast rounds to a rewritable register without changing acceptor state.

Recovery is best described in terms of possible decided values.
A later prepare recovers the unique maximum, or any tied maximum, at the highest fast ballot it observes.
We validate the resulting protocol with a TLA+ model checked by TLC.

\paragraph{Future work.}
Natural next steps are flexible quorums~\cite{howard_icdcn21} and reconfiguration for shared fast ballots.
