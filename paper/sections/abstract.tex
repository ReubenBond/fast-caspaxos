\begin{abstract}
CASPaxos is a linearizable rewritable register built on single-decree Paxos.
It achieves 1~RTT steady-state writes via a piggybacked prepare, but only for the proposer that already holds the next ballot.

\emph{Fast CASPaxos} lets the piggybacked prepare designate either a proposer-owned classic ballot or a shared fast ballot, bringing Fast Paxos-style fast rounds to the register setting without changing acceptor state.

In the fixed-quorum setting, recovery after a failed fast round reduces to recovering the unique maximum, or any tied maximum, among values at the highest fast ballot seen by a prepare quorum.
The register therefore commits in 1~RTT without a leader when contention is low and otherwise falls back to classic recovery.

We give the protocol, a short safety sketch, and a TLC-checked TLA\textsuperscript{+} specification.
\end{abstract}
